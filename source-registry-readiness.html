Readiness distinctions

  • Source review readiness: whether source objects are organized enough for human or later retrieval review.
  • Retrieval status: whether a source object says it was provided, retrieved, not retrieved, unavailable, or needs review.
  • Verification: not performed here. Retrieval status never proves a claim.
  • Unresolved source questions: open questions that must stay visible until answered elsewhere.
