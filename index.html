Structural rule

The brain is one body. Kernel, language, truth, memory, and action are aspects of that same body, not separate containers.

Focused expression

Focus is the current visible emphasis of the one body. It does not assign permanent worth to an aspect. It only states how the whole is presently showing itself.

E_f(B) = Σ wᵢ Aᵢ Σ wᵢ = 1 Aᵢ = aspect of B wᵢ = current visible emphasis
